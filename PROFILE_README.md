## Hi, I'm Jakob

I am an astrophysicist and Evolving Universe Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Astronomy & Astrophysics](https://science.psu.edu/astro) at The Pennsylvania State University. I spend my time studying the first stars, galaxies, galaxy clusters, and large-scale structure of the Universe using observations from the [James Webb Space Telescope (JWST)](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/).

I am a key member of the JWST [JADES](https://jades-survey.github.io/) team and the MIRI and NIRCam Science Teams. My contributions to extragalactic astronomy include helping discover [JADES-GS-z14-0](https://science.nasa.gov/blogs/webb/2024/05/30/nasas-james-webb-space-telescope-finds-most-distant-known-galaxy/), one of the most distant galaxies currently known at a redshift of $z > 14$.

Beyond research, I am also a visual artist working in photography, painting, digital art, and film — and I believe that sharing knowledge with others is one of the most important things I can do as a scientist.

---

### Research Interests

- High-redshift galaxy evolution and formation
- High-redshift galaxy clusters
- Large-scale structure of the Universe
- Observational extragalactic astronomy
